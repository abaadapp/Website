Create your website, showcase your products and services, and make your business easier to reach.

ABAAD Web is your business website inside ABAAD System. You build and run it from your ABAAD account, and it shows your products from where you run the rest of your business — no separate app, no separate account.

What do you get with ABAAD Web?

A website in your business name

A home page, extra pages and a navigation menu, built from the Website section of your dashboard.

A look you choose and customize

Choose how your header, hero, product cards, categories and footer look, and add your own images and text.

Your products on show

Your products appear straight from the system, with their images and categories, so you never enter them twice — and you choose whether to show their prices.

Contact on WhatsApp

Visitors message your business's WhatsApp number to ask or to order, and you follow up the conversation yourself.

Found in search

Set your website's title, description and sharing image as they appear in search engines.

Launch your website in three steps

  1. Create your ABAAD account, or log in to your existing one.
  2. Add your products and design your website from the Website section.
  3. Publish your website and share its link with your customers.
